About

Exploring intelligence that can act.

I am an aspiring AI researcher interested in embodied agents that connect perception and language with purposeful action.

My interests center on robotics, vision-language-action models, and generative models. I am especially curious about how generative learning can help robots acquire generalizable behaviors, reason over long-horizon tasks, and adapt when the physical world does not unfold as expected.
